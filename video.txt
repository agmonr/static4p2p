Design: Decentralized Social Network with Privacy + Lawful Accountability
Goal

Create a decentralized P2P social platform combining:

Instagram-style feed
WhatsApp-style messaging
Telegram-style channels
P2P distributed storage
End-to-end encryption
Local-first operation
Abuse reporting and lawful investigation capability

Principle:

Users control their data, the network has no single owner, but content has cryptographic accountability.
